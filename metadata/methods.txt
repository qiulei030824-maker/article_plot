## Methods: Phylogenetic Analysis of PF00168

Protein sequences containing PF00168 domains were retrieved from 33 species
using HMMER3. For phylogenetic reconstruction, 33 representative sequences were selected.
Multiple sequence alignment was performed using MAFFT (auto).
The alignment spanned 2596 positions (trimmed to 902 positions using
ClipKIT). A total of 902 parsimony-informative sites were retained.
Maximum likelihood phylogeny was inferred using IQ-TREE2 under the LG+G4 model
with 100 ultrafast bootstrap replicates.
